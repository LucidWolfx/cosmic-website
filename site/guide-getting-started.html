GETTING STARTED

Find your first steps and learn how account access differs from whitelist approval.

Guide draft for review. Approved server rules take precedence.

Start with About and Community to understand Cosmic. Read the current community rules before applying. Cosmic is an 18+ serious roleplay community.

After Discord sign-in, your portal account is an applicant account. You can prepare a draft when the backend is configured. Submissions stay closed until the owner announces an opening.

Use My Applications to read the decision and any requested changes. An approved application unlocks website member resources. A separate server-side whitelist must confirm that you can join FiveM.
