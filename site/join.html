What you can prepare

A character concept, a reason you want to join Cosmic, and examples of how you would handle shared roleplay. Final eligibility requirements will be set by the team.

After you are approved

The portal unlocks member resources and additional requests. Portal approval does not automatically add Discord roles or authorize a FiveM connection.

Is the whitelist open yet?

This build does not announce an opening date. Intake is closed by default and must be opened by the community owner.

Do I need a separate website password?

This portal uses Discord sign-in. Cosmic does not ask you to enter your Discord password on this website.

Does signing in make me a member?

No. A new account starts as an applicant. Whitelist approval and any server activation happen separately.

Can I save my application?

Once the backend is configured, you can save a private draft and return to it. Submission is available only when the team opens intake.
