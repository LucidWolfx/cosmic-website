FINDING YOUR CIRCLE

Turn a shared idea into an organization with its own identity.

Guide draft for review. Approved server rules take precedence.

An organization needs a shared purpose, a recognizable identity, and a reason to interact with people outside its membership. A social club, car crew, or other group can each create a different kind of roleplay.

Prepare your concept, expected activities, and contact person before requesting recognition. Criminal organizations remain subject to the approved server rules and any organization-specific limits.

Recognition, roster management, ranks, and in-game permissions are not automatically granted by website approval.
